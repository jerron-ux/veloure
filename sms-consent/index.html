1. Exactly how a subscriber opts in

We never add anyone to a text list from a purchased number, a lead list, or a paper form. The only way to opt in is checking an unchecked SMS-consent checkbox placed directly beside the phone number field on our own web forms -- the "Talk to an advisor" scheduler and the "Start an application" qualifier at this website and the application form. The box is never pre-checked and submission is blocked until the subscriber ticks it themselves. The checkbox text (consent version veloure-web-consent-v1-2026-11-08) reads, verbatim:

“By providing my phone number and checking this box, I agree to receive SMS marketing and informational text messages from VELOURÉ (VELOURÉ LLC), including funding options and offers, appointment confirmations, document requests and application status updates. You are opting into marketing texts from VELOURÉ. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message frequency may vary. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help, call (888) 984-9555 or email support@orioncapitalfunding.com. We will not share mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.”

We record the consent wording above, a timestamp, the subscriber's IP address and the page URL with every opt-in, so each consent is individually provable.

4. Frequency, cost and consent terms

  • Message frequency may vary. Most subscribers hear from us a handful of times while their request is active; frequency depends on where a subscriber is in their funding process.
  • Msg & data rates may apply. Standard message and data rates may apply from the subscriber's wireless carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent is never a condition of any purchase or service. Declining to check the box does not affect eligibility for funding, buildout services, or anything else we offer.
  • We will not share a subscriber's mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

5. How to opt out or get help

Every message we send carries opt-out and help instructions. Reply directly to any text we send:

STOP (also UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) — stops all further texts immediately. Auto-reply: “You're unsubscribed from VELOURÉ texts and won't receive more. Reply START to opt back in.”
START (also UNSTOP) — opts back in at any time. Auto-reply: “You're opted back in to VELOURÉ texts. Reply STOP any time to opt out.”
HELP — returns support contact info. Auto-reply: “Orion Capital Funding: msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Help: support@orioncapitalfunding.com or (888) 984-9555.”

A subscriber can also call (888) 984-9555 or email support@orioncapitalfunding.com any time to opt out or ask for help -- never required to text first.
