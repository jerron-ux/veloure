01 — What we do

Two services.
One plan built around your situation.

Some clients need both. Some need one. We start by working out which — then we do the work with you and keep you in the loop the whole way.

Service 01

Business & Personal Funding

We're a brokerage — we don't lend, we match. We review your profile, package your file the way lenders want to see it, and connect you with funding options that fit. You make every final call.

We are not a lender. Approval, amounts, and terms are decided by lenders based on their own criteria.

03 — How it works

Getting funded isn't luck.
It's preparation.

Three stages, in order. You'll always know which one you're in.

Stage 01

Initial consultation

We start with a conversation about where you are today — your goals, your business, and your numbers. No fee, no pressure, no obligation to continue.

Stage 02

Your plan

We map out a clear plan across whichever areas apply to you — business setup and funding. You'll know what we're doing and why at every step.

Stage 03

We get to work

We put the plan into action: structuring your business, assembling your documentation, and preparing your file for lenders. Regular updates, and a real person to talk to.

What it is not

Not a lender. Not an overnight fix. Not a guaranteed number. A structured process, with a person on your file who answers the phone.
