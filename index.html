01  //  Funding brokerage

The Funding File

2026  /  Brokerage  /  Business + personal

Your business, packaged the way capital reads it — and placed with lenders who fund your profile, not the profile on their landing page.

What we place

  • Term loans and business lines of credit
  • Working capital and revenue-based advances
  • Equipment and vehicle financing
  • Business credit cards and 0% introductory lines
  • Personal funding routes for owners and founders
  • Real-estate-backed and SBA-style programs through partner lenders

How a file moves

  1. A fifteen-minute call. We read the business the way a lender will.
  2. We build the file: statements, structure, story, use of funds.
  3. We place it with the lenders whose box you actually fit.
  4. Offers come back. We read the terms with you. You choose.

We broker. We do not lend, and no approval, amount or timeline is guaranteed — lenders decide on their own criteria. We tell you where you stand before anyone pulls anything.

02  //  Business buildout

The Buildout

2026  /  Structure  /  Weeks, not quarters

The foundation lenders check before they say yes — built in the order they check it.

What gets built

  • Entity formation, EIN, operating documents, registered agent
  • Business banking and merchant processing, opened and seasoned
  • Business credit profile: registrations, bureaus, first tradelines
  • Presence that passes a lender's five-minute check: domain, site, email, phone, listings
  • A fundability review — what a lender sees today, and what changes it

Who it is for

  • New companies that want to be fundable from day one
  • Operating businesses that keep getting declined and don't know why
  • Owners separating personal and business credit before they scale

Buildout is advisory and administrative work on your company's structure and records. It is not legal or tax advice, and it does not guarantee any lender's decision.

03  //  Lender packaging

Lender-Ready Packaging

2026  /  Documentation  /  Every file we place

A lender gives your file about four minutes. We make sure those four minutes answer every question before it is asked.

Inside the package

  • Bank statements with the story read for them: deposits, balances, seasonality
  • Financials, tax returns and a clean ownership record
  • A one-page summary: who you are, what you need, how it gets repaid
  • Use-of-funds and collateral, where it applies
  • Everything named, ordered and dated the way underwriters expect

Why it matters

Most declines are not about the business. They are about a file that made the underwriter work. A packaged file gets read, gets priced better, and gets answered faster.

04  //  Credit architecture

Credit, Architected

2026  /  Profile  /  Before the application

Lenders read a profile before they read a pitch. We build the business profile in sequence and show you, plainly, what the personal one says.

Business side

  • Registrations and bureau files established and matched to your entity
  • Starter tradelines in the order that reports
  • Utilisation, payment cadence and limits planned, not improvised
  • A timeline from first tradeline to bank-grade products

Personal side

  • A readiness read: what a lender will see, what will stop them, what to do first
  • Timing advice — when to apply, when to wait

We do not dispute, "repair" or alter credit history, and we make no promises about scores. We show you what lenders will see and how to prepare for it.
