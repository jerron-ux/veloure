1. The program

These terms govern text messages (SMS and MMS) sent by VELOURÉ LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company doing business as VELOURÉ ("VELOURÉ," "we," "us"), from our business telephone numbers (the "VELOURÉ Funding Consultation Texts" program). By opting in you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

Messages are conversational and relate to your inquiry: scheduling, confirming or rescheduling a consultation; following up on a consultation request; reminders about calls you booked; requests for information or documents you agreed to provide; and status updates on your file. Where you have asked us to, we may also tell you about VELOURÉ services relevant to your situation.

VELOURÉ is a brokerage. We do not lend, and a text from us is never an offer, approval or guarantee of credit. Approval, amounts, and terms are decided by lenders based on their own criteria.

2. How you opt in

You join the program only by one of these actions:

  • Submitting the "Talk to an advisor" or "Start an application" form on this website and ticking the unchecked-by-default consent box, which reads: "I agree to receive calls and texts from VELOURÉ about my inquiry at the number provided, including by automated technology. Consent is not a condition of service. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help." We record the date, time, IP address and the exact wording you agreed to.
  • Asking a member of our team, in writing or on a recorded call, to text you at your number. We note the request on your file.

Consent is not a condition of purchasing or receiving any VELOURÉ service. We do not buy phone lists, and we do not text people who have not opted in with us directly.

3. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies and depends on where your inquiry stands; during an active inquiry you may receive several messages in a week, and none at all once your file is closed. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. VELOURÉ does not charge for text messages.

4. How to stop (STOP)

Reply STOP to any VELOURÉ text to opt out. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further texts will be sent to that number unless you opt in again (for example by replying START or by submitting a new form). You can also opt out by emailing support@orioncapitalfunding.com or calling (888) 984-9555. Opting out of texts does not affect emails or calls you have separately agreed to; tell us if you want those to stop as well.

5. How to get help (HELP)
